## How the systems connect

**The Anchor Audit Lab accompanies the current AlphaGenome rework, submitted to BMC Bioinformatics on 29 October 2026 and in technical check.** It makes a declared contact-map anchoring path inspectable through persistence-diagram distances, ranking bounds and unresolved decisions. Synthetic examples, frozen aggregate study summaries and user-supplied diagrams are identified separately. The paired IMR-90 test evaluates a second output track of the same model; it is not independent-model or experimental validation. The Python toolkit supports larger diagrams; the earlier allele explorer preserves the preceding background-profile comparison. The research README identifies verification material and the complete study archive, with their reproduction scope and output terms. The earlier manuscript and data DOIs do not identify the new rework or its toolkit.

**Coastal Water Resilience combines two research directions.** It adapts spatial exposure and spillover diagnostics from the CRT framework alongside directed source/sink accounting informed by the network-contagion research. Its default projected-impact screens and ten-parameter plans use official public station records, County evacuation zones, USGS terrain, and NOAA/NWS observations and forecasts. Census populations and annual treatment flows supply geographic and facility context. Phase-specific explanations connect parameters to decisions, timing, and escalation. Public data can support up to eight planning dimensions when the required inputs and comparison groups are available; probabilistic free-energy and hydraulic leakage remain unknown. Geographic exposure screens do not establish hydraulic connectivity, outages, or service losses. A separate synthetic exercise demonstrates network behavior.

**The wider [Public Data Consoles collection](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/) extends the ten-parameter planning approach across 32 dashboards.** Plans use each console's supported public observations or published model outputs, distinguish operational evidence from illustrative scenarios, and identify the inputs needed for unresolved dimensions. Action cards explain the parameter's relevance, review timing, and escalation steps for public users and professional operators. The domain-specific diagnostics are documented adaptations of the framework.

**Resilience Telemetry translates the directed-contagion work into an interactive monitoring tool.** Its live-data workflow, ridge VAR, historical replay and transmitter-stability displays extend the presentation to a different setting. The manuscript's non-negative VAR and interdiction experiments remain in the research repository; the [research guide](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/resilience-telemetry/wiki.html#s8b) explains the differences.

**Autonomic Telemetry showcases research engineering in wearable sensing.** Its [source](https://github.com/patrickiben/autonomic-telemetry) and [live interface](https://patrickiben.github.io/autonomic-telemetry/) demonstrate device adapters, a local bridge, signal-quality contracts and tested numerical views. A specific manuscript correspondence is not assigned here.

## Versions and citations

- **Manuscript/preprint links** identify the paper itself. “Latest preprint” uses the Zenodo concept DOI, which follows future versions. “Latest manuscript” identifies the current AlphaGenome rework's GitHub author manuscript, submitted to BMC Bioinformatics on 29 October 2026 and not accepted; its research folder records the version. The GitHub-hosted author drafts do not currently have new preprint DOIs.
- **Software/data DOIs** identify archived research artifacts. They should be cited separately from the manuscript. Current GitHub branches can include later maintenance fixes; use the archived release to reproduce that version.
- **Demonstrations and adaptations** illustrate or transfer selected methods. Their data, estimands and implementation can differ from the manuscript; each project's documentation explains its scope.
- **Copyright and terms** remain specific to each artifact. MIT code licenses do not automatically apply to manuscript PDFs. AlphaGenome-derived outputs retain their separate output terms.
