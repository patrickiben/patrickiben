### Patrick Iben, MS · Applied mathematics, research engineering, predictive analytics, and clinical biostatistics

I work to eliminate needless ambiguity, risk, and suffering in decisions that rest on data. I build mathematical frameworks and turn uncertain signals into inspectable, cross-validated systems across clinical trial design and analysis, causal and network inference, model and AI evaluation, and decision support built on public and sensor data. I look for agreement across independent lines of evidence, and for the shared structure that connects fields that seem unrelated, so a method proven in one setting can be tested honestly in another.

7 years of clinical biostatistics in contract research (early-phase clinical pharmacology, CDISC/ADaM, PK/NCA) and 12 years in research. Remote US.

#### Selected work

**Research engineering and systems architecture:** translating methods across domains, integrating data and models, and building tools whose assumptions and evidence can be inspected.

- **[Coastal Water Resilience](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/coastal-water-resilience/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/public-data-consoles/tree/main/consoles/coastal-water-resilience)): Connects my [CRT framework](https://github.com/patrickiben/crt-design-demo) and [network-contagion research](https://github.com/patrickiben/directed-asymmetry-contagion) to infrastructure planning. Real public station records, terrain, evacuation zones, Census context, and NOAA/NWS observations and forecasts support projected-impact screens and ten-parameter response plans. Actions explain why each parameter matters at that phase, with timing and escalation steps. Unsupported dimensions stay explicit; the synthetic exercise is separately labeled.

- **[Public Data Consoles](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/public-data-consoles)): 36 browser dashboards spanning hazards, public health, access to care, and markets, including [NHS Scotland Strain](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/nhs-scotland-strain/), [U.S. Grid Strain](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/us-grid-strain/), [River Flood Propagation](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/river-flood-propagation/), [Community Resilience](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/community-resilience/), and three place finders: [Food Desert Oases](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/food-desert-oases/), [Free Clinic Finder](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/free-clinic-finder/), and [Free Public Bathroom Finder](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/public-bathroom-finder/). Visitors need no API key. Public feeds and scheduled data publication support dated, source-aware displays; all 36 include ten-parameter response plans with priorities, escalation, and plain-language explanations for public users and professional operators.

- **[Autonomic Telemetry](https://patrickiben.github.io/autonomic-telemetry/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/autonomic-telemetry)): Wearable sensing from Bluetooth devices through an in-memory Mac bridge to a mobile browser. Work/Rest views combine a personal reference with explicit signal quality, freshness, and source coverage. The [Signal Lab](https://patrickiben.github.io/autonomic-telemetry/Autonomic_Telemetry_Signal_Lab.html) adds replayable numerical views with analytic and regression checks. Live wearable input runs through the local bridge.

- **[Resilience Telemetry](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/resilience-telemetry/)** ([research guide](https://patrickiben.github.io/public-data-consoles/consoles/resilience-telemetry/wiki.html#s8b)): An application of my directed-contagion research to cross-asset volatility networks: spillover analysis, transmitter stability, historical replay, and held-out comparisons against loudness and persistence baselines. Public-data ingestion and the documented method correspondence make the adaptation inspectable.

- **[myco-fusion](https://patrickiben.github.io/myco-fusion/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/myco-fusion)): How far a phone camera can go toward a reflectance spectrum: a local research workbench and a reproducible simulation study on measured spectra. On 532 natural-object samples, a simulated camera met both draft targets for 83% when calibrated on similar objects and 60% with a color chart; measured curves from six iPhones gave similar results. No phone photo or wild fungus was tested, and it is not for judging whether anything is safe to eat.

**Clinical trial tools:** worked examples, templates, apps and checks for biostatistics consultants and employees, from sample size to CSR text.

- **[Trial biostatistics toolkit](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/biometrics-ai-portfolio)): open-source tools, templates and worked examples across study planning, programming, operations and reporting, focused on early-phase clinical pharmacology. All 23 modules open from a [browser catalog](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/toolkit_launcher/index.html); core study setup and analyses need no local R, Python, account or download. Each page states what was executed (runs, parse-checked, not executed); example data are synthetic, and nothing is validated or regulatory.
  - [Flagship worked example](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/study/): one synthetic 2x2 crossover bioequivalence study (N = 40), from sample size and randomization through the SAP, ADaM (admiral), NCA (PKNCA), the ICH M13A analysis, TLFs, and CSR Section 11-style text, regenerated by one script in CI. QC is a same-author re-derivation, not independent double programming.
  - [TRIALMON safety review](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/sasr_monitoring_wiki/): a Safety Review Committee packet and Shiny console for a synthetic first-in-human dose escalation, with a Hy's Law grid that checks whether each flag holds under alternative SAP conventions. Not validated; author sign-off pending.
  - [QC harness](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/validation_harness/): ten gates, including a same-author recomputation of the PK, bioequivalence, and adverse-event results and a seeded-defect check of 18 defects registered in advance (counts for that list, not a detection rate), in which only the recomputation caught the seven numeric and counting defects. The ledger records the eight automated checks as passing and the two human-review gates (QC review and AI-use accountability) as reviewed; the checks cover the toolkit's own libraries and synthetic data, not any study.
  - Method transfers from my research: an exploratory [residual-carryover study](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/sim_lifecycle/carryover_oc/) of the ICH M13A 5% pre-dose rule, following the design of my CRT simulation study; an exploratory [C-QTc hysteresis check](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/cqtc_hysteresis/), a small formalization of the standard check on the established signed loop area, framed from my directed-contagion work; and the seeded-defect check (established error seeding, adapted from the teeth check in my consilience engine) and the Hy's Law grid (a structured sensitivity analysis, adapted from its method-invariance grid).
  - SAS and R TLF libraries (221 programs per language across five study designs; all 221 R programs execute on synthetic fixtures, native SAS not executed) and four R Shiny apps that run in the browser, with live loading status and a pre-deploy browser test: [TRIALMON console](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/apps/trialmon/), [IRT/RTSM UAT tracker](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/apps/uat_irt/), [Study Lifecycle Monitor](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/apps/lifecycle_dashboard/), and the [vaccine analysis workspace](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/apps/vaccine/), which maps study CSVs or loads a fictional example and runs six immunogenicity and reactogenicity outputs with no installation or account. The [operational risk signals](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/apps/risk/) and [hours reconciliation](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/apps/hours/) apps also run in the browser with mapped study inputs.
  - [eCRF review workspace](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/ecrf_pipeline/docs/) ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/biometrics-ai-portfolio/tree/main/ecrf_pipeline)): four browser tools for your own CSV extracts: coding QC assist, de-identification review assist, participant risk screening, and anomaly and drift screening. Each tool starts blank; no client data or licensed MedDRA dictionary is bundled, and the optional example is synthetic. The [evaluation](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/ecrf_pipeline/docs/evaluation.html) documents the original implementation's measured failures (the de-identification stand-in left 108 of 308 identifiers readable, the full model 91) and a pre-registered within-sibling check, adapted from my AlphaGenome audit, that routes both term families to human coding. Not validated. The apps moved here from the archived [ecrf-pipeline-apps](https://github.com/patrickiben/ecrf-pipeline-apps) repository, whose pages now redirect.
  - [Point-and-click setup](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/TOOLKIT_SETUP.html): an optional local control panel for private projects, numerical examples, QC checks and generated study packages, with guided forms that map CSV inputs to the TLF libraries and monitoring checks.
  - Operational tools, no AI required: [Open Study Tracker](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/open_tracker/index.html), a [coverage and continuity toolkit](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/sasr_onleave_wiki/Continuity_Toolkit.html), and a [find-hours reconciliation](https://patrickiben.github.io/biometrics-ai-portfolio/budget_reconciliation/quarto/hours-guide.html) for effort only.

- **[catmech](https://github.com/patrickiben/catmech)**: An R package (version 1.0.1) for regression when recorded values may be wrong and an audit has checked a subset against the source. Its own tests on public data found that simple linear multiple imputation given the recorded value matched or beat the recording-error model the package added, so linear imputation is the recommended start. The clustered-audit option is experimental, and the audit-planning function failed a rule fixed in advance. Not on CRAN, with no outside peer review or clinical validation.

- **[calibrated-clinical-ml](https://github.com/patrickiben/calibrated-clinical-ml)**: From-scratch calibration and decision-curve evaluation against a synthetic oracle; demonstrates how identical AUC can conceal different probability calibration.

**Signal instruments and experiments:** browser tools that read a body, sound, or motion signal and label what is measured, inferred, or simulated.

- **[Body Signal Labs](https://patrickiben.github.io/body-signal-labs/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/body-signal-labs)): Browser scenes that follow signals from the body and say what was measured and what was inferred. [Endless Cockpit](https://patrickiben.github.io/body-signal-labs/endless-cockpit.html) sets riding speed through nine procedural scenes from pedaling cadence (a Bluetooth sensor, a smart trainer, or an on-screen pedal) and exports sessions as TCX activity files. [Biofeedback Path Cockpit](https://patrickiben.github.io/body-signal-labs/biofeedback-path-cockpit.html) adds heart-rate and Muse S EEG inputs, a paced-breathing recovery view, and a heart-rate-variability coherence game, and uses Autonomic Telemetry's engine for its heart-shift score. Sensors report their connection state, and demos and replays are labeled. No page is a medical device, and [no real cadence sensor or heart-rate strap has connected to either cockpit yet](https://github.com/patrickiben/body-signal-labs/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

- **[Generative AV Labs](https://patrickiben.github.io/generative-av-labs/)** ([source](https://github.com/patrickiben/generative-av-labs)): Browser instruments for light and sound that run with no helper app or server; audio, MIDI, and pictures stay in the browser. [Nebula](https://patrickiben.github.io/generative-av-labs/nebula.html) draws an electronic drum kit's audio and MIDI as a twelve-second spiral and matches a picture to a sound by sliced optimal transport. [Displacer](https://patrickiben.github.io/generative-av-labs/displacer.html) blends two sounds by moving energy along frequency with an optimal-transport map instead of a crossfade. [Chimera Synth](https://patrickiben.github.io/generative-av-labs/chimera.html) grows a bass voice from a 20-gene genome. [Enter](https://patrickiben.github.io/generative-av-labs/enter.html) turns a picture into a depth relief to walk through and a sound into a map to fly through; it uses a depth-estimation model, downloaded only on request, and the other four use no model. [Detail Forge Analog](https://patrickiben.github.io/generative-av-labs/analog.html) adds a synthesized high-frequency layer that is procedural, not recovered audio. [No real drum kit has been used with Nebula yet](https://github.com/patrickiben/generative-av-labs/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

- **[PetRand](https://github.com/patrickiben/petrand)** ([motion game](https://patrickiben.github.io/petrand/)): An experimental entropy source designed around a motorized cat-toy ball bouncing in a small arena, with the tools to test whether it produces unpredictable numbers: two health tests that follow the designs in NIST SP 800-90B, an entropy credit per sample, and HMAC-SHA-256 mixing into a 32-byte seed. Research prototype: no physical arena has been built or measured, runs use a labeled simulator unless a camera or microphone is selected, the entropy credit is a placeholder, and nothing is certified.

#### AI evaluation and grounding

Small, runnable tools for examining model-output reliability and service behavior:

- **[numguard](https://github.com/patrickiben/numguard)**: Deterministic checks of extracted numeric values against allowed facts, required facts, and a finite phrase denylist; JSON/JUnit export for CI.

- **[tool-grounded-agent](https://github.com/patrickiben/tool-grounded-agent)**: A scripted planner demonstrates tool-ledger provenance and a grounding backstop. The harness includes executable known-gap tests; it does not run a language model.

- **[grounded-rag](https://github.com/patrickiben/grounded-rag)**: Lexical retrieval and an extractive answerer with checks for numeric membership and lexical support. A runnable evaluation prototype.

- **[guarded-llm-service](https://github.com/patrickiben/guarded-llm-service)**: The grounding guard served over FastAPI with Docker, CI, and Prometheus-style metrics.
